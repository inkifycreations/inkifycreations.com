Product Care Guide

Print Washing Care Guidelines

Follow these guidelines to keep your customized sublimation prints looking vibrant and fresh for years.

Bespoke T-Shirts

  • Machine wash cold, inside-out with similar fabrics.
  • Use mild detergent. Avoid bleach and fabric softeners.
  • Tumble dry on low heat or hang dry in shade.
  • Do not dry clean or iron directly over the printed artwork.

Ceramic Mugs

  • Hand wash recommended with a soft sponge and warm water.
  • Microwave safe for standard warming.
  • Dishwasher safe, but top-rack placement on gentle cycle is best.
  • Do not scrub with metal scourers or harsh abrasives.

Custom Bottles & Tumblers

  • Hand wash only using warm soapy water.
  • Clean interior with a soft bottle brush.
  • Never microwave (metal construction warning).
  • Do not wash in dishwasher (heat degrades print and seals).
